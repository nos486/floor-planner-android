# Privacy Policy — Floor Planner

_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

Floor Planner (`org.geekio.floorplanner`) does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

- The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not request internet access.
- Your floor plans are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage.
- When you open, save or export a file, the app only reads or writes the file you pick in the system file picker.
- Uninstalling the app deletes its saved data.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/nos486/floor-planner-android/issues
