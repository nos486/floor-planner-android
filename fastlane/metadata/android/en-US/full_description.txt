Floor Planner is a simple, practical way to sketch the plan of a home, an apartment or a single room on your phone.

Draw walls with your finger and the app finds the rooms for you, showing the area of each room in square meters and the total area of the plan.

DRAW
• Drag to draw walls. Ends snap to other walls, to the grid and to 45° angles
• See the length of every wall while you draw
• Add doors (with their swing) and windows by tapping on a wall

EDIT
• Move walls and corners; joined walls follow
• Set an exact wall length, wall thickness, door or window width
• Split walls, flip doors, rename rooms
• Undo and redo

ROOMS AND AREA
• Rooms are detected automatically
• Area of each room and total area in m²

SAVE AND SHARE
• Your plan is saved automatically
• Open and save projects as JSON files
• Export the plan as an SVG image

No account, no ads and no internet connection needed. Your plans stay on your device.
