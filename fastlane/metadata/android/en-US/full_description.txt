Floor Planner is a simple, practical way to sketch the plan of a home, an apartment or a single room on your phone.

Draw walls with your finger and the app finds the rooms for you, showing the area of each room and the total area of the plan.

DRAW
• Drag to draw walls. Ends snap to other walls, to the grid and to 45° angles
• Curved walls, low walls (counters, railings) and columns
• Add doors (single, double or sliding) and windows by tapping on a wall
• Several floors, with the floor below shown faintly to line walls up
• Trace over a photo of a paper plan after setting its scale
• Start from an empty plan, a ready-made apartment or a furnished two-storey sample home

FURNISH
• Furniture and fixtures for every room, plus stairs, columns and a fireplace
• Electrical and plumbing points: sockets, switches, lights, water, drains, gas, radiators
• Move, rotate and resize them; they snap against the walls
• Give each room a colour and a floor: parquet, tiles, stone or carpet

SEE IT
• 3D view you can turn and zoom
• Sunlight: see where the sun falls through the windows in any month and hour, and the hours of sun in each room
• Actual size: zoom to 1:1 and hold the phone against a real object
• Layers to show or hide furniture, electrical, plumbing, measurements and notes

CHECK IT
• Plan check: doors that hit furniture, furniture in walls or in front of windows, narrow rooms, rooms without a door or window
• Materials: floor covering, wall paint and skirting quantities and costs
• Home buyer: usable and gross area, price per m², and a side-by-side comparison of homes
• Versions: save the plan before a renovation and compare what changed

EDIT
• Move walls and corners; joined walls follow
• Set an exact wall length, thickness, height and curve, door or window width
• Select several things at once to move, copy, rotate, mirror or delete them
• Undo and redo

MEASURE AND NOTE
• Overall width and height around the plan
• Measure tool for your own dimension lines
• Text notes with arrows
• Metres or feet and inches

SAVE AND SHARE
• Keep as many projects as you like; each one is saved automatically
• Export as PNG, PDF (one page per floor, with a room schedule and scale bar), SVG or DXF for CAD
• Share the image or PDF straight to any app
• Open and save projects as JSON files

No account, no ads and no internet connection needed. Your plans stay on your device.
